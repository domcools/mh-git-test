Flagship event

Teenage Cancer Trust Week at the Royal Albert Hall

When15 to 21 March 2027
WhereRoyal Albert Hall, London
GroupPrivate Boxes of 4, 8 & 12

For more than 25 years, these concerts have brought some of the biggest names in music and comedy to one of London's most iconic venues. Paul McCartney, Eric Clapton, Oasis, The Cure, Ed Sheeran, Peter Kaye and Alan Carr have all played here for Teenage Cancer Trust. Marshall Harrison arranges screen advertising and private box hospitality through the week, so your guests are looked after from arrival to encore.

An Iconic London Venue.

Some venues build a vibe for the night before anyone takes the stage. The Royal Albert Hall does that. The scale, the history, the sound, the sense of occasion — it all adds up. Add a private box, full hospitality and a bill curated for one of the UK's most loved charity music weeks, and you have the kind of evening people remember, with every booking helping fund Teenage Cancer Trust's specialist work.

A night with purpose

Teenage Cancer Trust at the Royal Albert Hall has raised more than £36m since the concerts began, helping fund specialist nurses, hospital units and support services across the UK. Every booking through Marshall Harrison helps continue that work, while giving your guests a night they will genuinely remember.
