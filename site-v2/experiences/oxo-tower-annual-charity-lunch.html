Flagship event

The OXO Tower Charity Christmas Lunch

WhenFriday 27th November
WhereOXO Tower, London
GroupTables of 10

A festive charity lunch overlooking the Thames, bringing together great hospitality, familiar faces from sport and entertainment, a live auction and one of London's most memorable dining rooms.

With just 160 guests, tables are limited and the atmosphere is personal. It is the kind of lunch that works beautifully for clients, colleagues and friends: generous, lively, social and all in support of causes that matter.

Enquire

A room full of personality

Spend an afternoon full of celebrity Christmas cheer and entertainment with our sporting hosts Phil Tufnell, Alan Brazil, Ronnie Irani, Paul Merson, Matt Dawson, Dean Saunders and Ray Parlour. We build on the line up each year, but the spirit of the lunch stays the same.

There'll be Q&As, live performances, table-side entertainment and the chance to meet the people who make the afternoon feel special.

Table of 10 at the OXO Tower

Wine and drinks with lunch
Sporting and entertainment guests in the room

Company recognition, where included in the package
Your booking helps raise funds for charitable causes

Causes that matter

The Annual Charity Lunch has supported charities including Guy's & Barts and the IJF.

Every table helps turn a memorable festive lunch into meaningful support for organisations doing important work, from exceptional care in London hospitals to support for jockeys and their families when injury changes everything.
