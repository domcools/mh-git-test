Flagship event

The Alan Brazil talkSPORT Charity Golf Series

When10–11 September 2026
WhereBrocket Hall, Hertfordshire
GroupTeams of 4

Two days of golf, hospitality and sporting stories at Brocket Hall, hosted by Alan Brazil and friends. Where you can have a VIP golfing partner as part of your team. Previous hosts include Teddy Sheringham, Harry Redknopp, Glenn Hoddle, and Ken Doherty. Play one of England's great country-estate golf venues, entertain clients or colleagues, spend the day alongside familiar names from sport and broadcasting, and help raise funds for the IJF and Addenbrooke Hospital.

Enquire

Two days of golf you'll never forget

Set in the Hertfordshire countryside, Brocket Hall gives the event the setting it deserves, with talkSPORT being recorded live from the event. Across the two days, guests play championship golf on the estate's Melbourne and Palmerston courses, with breakfast on arrival, on-course hospitality, prizes and evening dining as part of the experience.

It is a strong invitation for clients, colleagues and friends: good golf, great company, and a day hosted with real character. Former Scotland international Alan Brazil, Ipswich Town favourite and long-time talkSPORT Breakfast host, brings the kind of warmth, humour and sporting storytelling that turns a golf day into something more memorable, with plenty to talk about long after the final putt.

Each year, the event attracts names from football, rugby, cricket, racing and broadcasting, creating a room and course full of personality. Bring a team, entertain people properly, and be part of a charity golf series built around sport, stories and causes that matter.
